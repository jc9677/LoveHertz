When Clicks Become Pitch

Below about 20 Hz, you hear separate clicks. Push frequency higher and your ear starts hearing a stable tone. This demo uses three impulse-train generators and a single global frequency control.

Global Frequency

Log scale from 1 Hz to 20,000 Hz. All generators scale together while preserving their relative ratios.
